CHRONOMANCALA

seeds of the antikythera

Two players sow grains around the wheel.
Every grain turns the bronze gears.
Every moon, the mechanism grants an omen.

Sow — lift every grain from one of your houses; they fall one by one around the ring.
Granary — land your last grain here and sow again.
Capture — land in an empty house of yours and seize the opposite house.
Omens — each moon (24 grains) the gears bestow a blessing. Beware the eclipse.
